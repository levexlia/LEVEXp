# Scene 11 — licenses and attribution

No-road final of the scene 10 zigzag for LEVEXp. The geodesic-dome
heroes are unchanged from scene 9 (original drawn SVG). The layout
and badge seats are the scene 10 variant B (no road). Progress is
shown with badge states, not a path. No light-bridges, no dotted
road, no traced packs, no pines, no Figma Mini Town, no Buggy Dark Mode.

## Original / LEVEX
- Landmark artwork is the scene 9 shaded interiors (hobbit-pod,
  floating book, puffy tree with fruits, gear + hammer, hex-logo
  crystal) and flora (neon lamps). See scene 9 and scene 10 LICENSES.md.
- Layout, hex-island grid, and outer-vertex badge seats come from
  scene 10 (original work).
- New in this scene: the official map has no road; the phone mock
  uses only badge states (and a soft current-dome glow) for progress.
- Phone mock UI follows the existing LEVEXp kit. Avatar from
  `/workspace/levex_design/kit/`.
- Map-kit badges and nav icons are from the existing LEVEXp map kit.
- Nunito is used in the mock (SIL Open Font License).

## Sphere icons `sp_finance.svg`, `sp_relationships.svg` (2026-11, LEVEX artist)
- Original work, drawn from scratch in SVG code (circles, ellipses, paths) by the
  LEVEX artist to match the existing `sp_*.svg` kit: 300×300 viewBox, flat fills
  in the kit palette (#7341ed / #5f36d9 violet, #25cbfd / #0ac0f9 cyan,
  #fec130 / #fe9e0f gold), dark outline #081233 with #0a1c46 inner lines, no animation.
- `sp_finance.svg`: a coin stack with a rising trend arrow.
- `sp_relationships.svg`: two interlocked rings (violet and cyan), «связь» between people.
- No third-party icon packs, stock art, or AI generation. Lucide (ISC) was
  considered as a reference but no Lucide paths are used, so no attribution is needed.
