# Scene 11 — licenses and attribution

No-road final of the scene 10 zigzag for LEVEXp. The geodesic-dome
heroes are unchanged from scene 9 (original drawn SVG). The layout
and badge seats are the scene 10 variant B (no road). Progress is
shown with badge states, not a path. No light-bridges, no dotted
road, no traced packs, no pines, no Figma Mini Town, no Buggy Dark Mode.
